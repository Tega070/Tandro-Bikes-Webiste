Gwaldi Pass
E-Bike Adventure

One of the most breathtaking alpine routes in Svaneti — from Mestia at 1,400m up to the Gwaldi Pass at ~2,025m.

Book This Tour

Ride one of the most breathtaking alpine routes in Svaneti. Starting from Mestia at 1,400m, this E-bike adventure climbs to the Gwaldi Pass at around 2,000–2,025m through gravel mountain roads and alpine trails. Throughout the ride you're surrounded by incredible panoramic scenery and authentic mountain landscapes unique to Svaneti. Designed for intermediate to advanced riders — not recommended for beginners.

Route Highlights

  • 360° Caucasus panorama — sweeping views of the Greater Caucasus range from the pass
  • Iconic mountain peaks — views of Ushba, Laila, Tetnuldi and Banguriani along the way
  • Remote alpine riding — gravel roads and mountain trails far from the tourist crowds
  • Historic Svan villages & medieval towers — the soul of Upper Svaneti on every corner
  • Long scenic descent back to Mestia — the rewarding ride down after the climb
  • Everything included — premium E-bike, helmet, local guide, photos & videos, water refill stops

35 km · 3–4 hours · ~600m climb · Gravel & alpine trails · Season: May 15 – November 15. Intermediate to advanced riders only.
